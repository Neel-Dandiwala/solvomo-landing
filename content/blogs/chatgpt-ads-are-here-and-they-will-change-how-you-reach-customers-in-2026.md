For years, digital advertising meant one of two things: interrupting people on social media, or bidding on keywords in Google. Both are well understood, both are increasingly crowded, and both are getting more expensive every year.

Now there is a third option. And it is fundamentally different from anything that has come before it.

OpenAI is rolling out advertising inside ChatGPT, complete with self-serve campaign tools, cost-per-click bidding, conversion tracking, and agency partner access. For marketers paying attention, this is one of the most significant new channels to emerge since social media advertising went mainstream.

> _ChatGPT users are not scrolling to pass the time. They are asking questions, comparing options, and making decisions. That changes what advertising can do._

---

## What Are ChatGPT Ads and How Do They Work?

ChatGPT ads are sponsored placements that appear inside ChatGPT conversations. Unlike a banner ad or a promoted post, they do not interrupt a user mid-scroll. They surface at the exact moment a user is actively researching a topic, weighing up options, or moving toward a purchase decision.

OpenAI is giving advertisers access to:

- Self-serve campaign management
- Cost-per-click (CPC) and CPM bidding
- Conversion tracking and performance reporting
- Agency and partner integrations

This is not a niche experiment. ChatGPT has over 300 million weekly active users as of early 2026. That is an audience the size of a major social platform, with a fundamentally different and more valuable type of attention.

---

## Why ChatGPT Ads Capture Higher Intent Than Google or Meta

The core difference between ChatGPT ads and every other ad format comes down to one word: intent.

Social media ads are built on interruption. A user is scrolling through Instagram or TikTok, not looking for anything in particular, and an ad appears. Some of them click. Most scroll past.

Search ads are better. A user types a query and advertisers bid to appear beside the result. Intent is present, but it is shallow. The user is looking, not yet deciding.

ChatGPT ads are different. A user opens ChatGPT because they have a specific question they want answered. They are mid-thought, mid-research, mid-decision. That is the moment your brand can show up.

|                      | Social Ads        | Search Ads    | ChatGPT Ads            |
| -------------------- | ----------------- | ------------- | ---------------------- |
| **Ad type**          | Interruption      | Keyword match | Decision moment        |
| **User state**       | Passive scrolling | Searching     | Actively deciding      |
| **Purchase intent**  | Low               | Medium        | **Highest**            |
| **Targeting method** | Behavioural       | Keyword       | Conversational context |

## The Questions Your Customers Are Already Asking ChatGPT

This is what makes ChatGPT advertising so compelling in practice. People are not using it to browse. They are using it to decide. These are the kinds of queries where ChatGPT ads are already appearing:

- "What is the best CRM for a small business?"
- "Which laptop should I buy for software development?"
- "How should I plan my marketing budget in 2026?"
- "What tools can help me improve ad performance?"
- "What project management software do marketing agencies use?"

Every one of those queries represents a person who is close to making a decision. That is the audience every advertiser wants to reach, and ChatGPT is where they are going.

The brands that move now will not just gain impressions. They will gain presence in the conversations that drive decisions.
